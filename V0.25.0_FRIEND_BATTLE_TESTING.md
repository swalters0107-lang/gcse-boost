# V0.25.0 Friend Battle testing

1. Run `supabase_v02500_friend_battles.sql` once after the V0.24.0 Social Foundations SQL.
2. Sign in on two student accounts that are accepted friends and have Friends enabled.
3. Student A opens Profile → Friends → Open Friends, chooses Student B, a subject and topic, then sends the challenge.
4. Student B opens Friends, accepts the challenge and completes all eight questions.
5. Student A opens the active battle and completes the identical eight questions.
6. Confirm the result uses score first and server-recorded completion time as the tie-breaker.
7. Use Rematch and confirm a fresh set of eight questions is created.
8. Confirm a fourth completed battle on the same day gives no extra battle reward.
9. Remove or block a friend and confirm unfinished battles between the students are cancelled.

Regression checks:

- Normal missions still use adaptive selection and lives.
- Friend Battles do not consume lives or open Recovery Rounds.
- Boss Battles and class lessons remain unchanged.
- Maths and Science Friend Battles never include questions above Grade 5.
