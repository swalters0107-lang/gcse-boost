# LevelUp10 V0.22.0 — Universal Mastery Engine

This release extends the proven per-skill mastery system across GCSE Boost.

## What changed
- Maths, Science and English curriculum screens now show unit/strand mastery and per-skill progress.
- History, Geography, German, Spanish, Drama, Citizenship, Sport Science, Catering and RE retain the same mastery engine introduced in V0.21.x.
- Every newly answered question records subject, curriculum area, mapped skill, difficulty, question fingerprint and correctness.
- Existing answer history is re-mapped where possible so prior progress is retained.
- Untested skills show `New` with no percentage.
- Tested skills progress through Developing, Secure and Mastered using attempts, accuracy, question variety and difficulty.
- Question selection continues to favour weaker/less-established skills while preserving repeat protection.

## Mastery thresholds
Skill: Developing after evidence begins; Secure requires at least 5 attempts, 70% accuracy and 4 distinct questions; Mastered requires at least 10 attempts, 85% accuracy, average difficulty 4.5+ and 8 distinct questions.

Area/unit: Developing from 3 attempts; Secure from 8 attempts at 70%+ across at least 2 skills; Mastered from 20 attempts at 85%+ with average difficulty 4.5+ across at least 4 skills.

No progress reset is required.
